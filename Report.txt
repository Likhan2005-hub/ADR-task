Title: ADR Identification Basics Report

1. Patient Profile:
* Patient Name: Rahul Sharma (Fictional)
* Age / Gender: 45 years / Male
* Medical History: Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

2. Presenting Symptoms & Timeline:
* The patient started taking a new blood pressure medication (Enalapril) 3 days ago.
* Within 24 hours of the first dose, the patient developed a persistent dry cough, throat irritation, and mild facial swelling.

3. Suspected Drug:
* Enalapril (ACE Inhibitor class)

4. Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Analysis & Conclusion:
* Reaction Type: ACE inhibitor-induced dry cough and angioedema symptoms.
* Causality Assessment: The symptoms appeared immediately after starting Enalapril and worsened with continued doses, which strongly links the drug to the reaction.
* Recommended Action: Stop the medication immediately under medical supervision and consult a doctor for an alternative blood pressure medication (like an ARB such as Losartan).
